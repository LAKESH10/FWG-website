Thirty years of keeping people and business safe.

FWG began in Seremban in 1995 with a security licence and a simple aim: give clients a security partner they can depend on. Today the group protects sites across Malaysia and India and has grown into trust services and property.

Our brand

FWG is an end-to-end security solution provider focused on protecting people, assets and business environments.

We combine experienced security operations with technology, so clients can focus on running their business.

FWG works with large organisations across the Asia-Pacific region. Clients stay because of our service, the quality of our people and our understanding of each site. We protect government premises, offices, homes, factories, banks, shopping complexes, retail outlets, construction sites and hospitals.

FWG operates in every region of Malaysia, and we aim to create value through responsible business in the communities where we work.

Escort security

Escort for VIPs and cash-in-transit

Secure logistics means protecting high-value goods at every point of the journey. We assess the risks first, then agree the right escort plan with you, and work as a partner to meet agreed delivery dates from origin to destination.

Our escort teams travel in unmarked vehicles backed by a dedicated logistics network. We escort private VIP vehicles and armoured cash-in-transit vehicles, and plan every assignment case by case. Armed escort is available on request.

ControlWritten standard operating procedures for every escort.
MonitoringVehicles send GPS data to FWG headquarters throughout the job.
SystemThe command centre checks each step against a checklist, so there is no single point of failure.
ResponseUpdates are escalated in real time if anything changes on the route.

At handover we check shipping documents against the actual stock and raise any shortfall at once. The job ends only when the goods reach the agreed recipient safely.

Home / Malaysia / Electronic security

Beyond guards, gates and guns

FWG designs, installs and monitors electronic security that works with your guards. Fewer blind spots, faster response, and clear records of who came and went.

Request a quote

CCTV & video surveillance

An end-to-end, enterprise-grade video surveillance system that is straightforward to test, validate and deploy. Cameras cover the whole facility, including hazardous areas and the perimeter, so threats are spotted early. They connect to access control, on-site alarms and two-way audio, with live streaming and video analytics. Careful camera placement reduces blind spots and can reduce the number of guards a site needs.

Visitor management system (iVMS)

A cloud-based system that replaces the paper sign-in book and tracks visitor access and movement. Hosts pre-schedule visits, so check-in takes seconds and reception is less busy. Includes photo capture, visitor passes, verification and real-time reports of who is on site, exportable to PDF.

Alarms & access control

Door access, turnstiles, lift access and CCTV integrated in real time. Card issuance and biometrics let you grant and change access rights as people's roles change.

GPS tracking & command centre

Vehicles and escort teams are tracked live from FWG headquarters. The command centre monitors routes and personnel and escalates incidents as they happen.

Central monitoring (CMS)

Remote monitoring of alarms and cameras across multiple sites from one control room. To confirm: what CMS stands for and its scope

Privacy notice

How FWG collects and uses personal data submitted through this website.

Draft outline only. Must be written and reviewed by FWG's legal adviser before launch.

Who we are

This website is operated by the FWG Group. The company responsible for your data depends on the country you contact: Freightwatch G Patomotoh Security Services Sdn Bhd (199501027914) in Malaysia, and Freightwatch G Security Services (India) Pvt. Ltd. in India. To confirm: the responsible entity for Singapore enquiries

What we collect

  • Quote requests: name, company, email, phone, site location, service needs, best time to call and any file you attach.
  • General enquiries: name, email, phone, country and your message.
  • Job applications: name, contact details, role of interest, CV and cover note.

Why we use it

To reply to your request, prepare a proposal, assess your application, and meet our legal obligations.

Your rights

You may ask to access or correct your personal data, or withdraw consent, by contacting the country team. Malaysia: Personal Data Protection Act 2010. India: Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023. Singapore: Personal Data Protection Act 2012.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and application data only as long as needed for the purpose it was given, or as the law requires. To supply: retention periods

Sharing and transfers

Data is shared within the FWG Group only to answer your request, for example with the country team you selected. To confirm: hosting location and any cross-border transfers

Cookies and storage

This site remembers your light or dark mode choice in your browser. It uses no advertising cookies. To confirm: analytics tools used at launch

Contact for data requests

Malaysia: info@fwg.my. India: info@fwgindia.com. To supply: data protection officer contact
