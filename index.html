Guarding & operations

Manned guarding you can audit

Well-trained, alert security officers are the core of a sound site security plan. FWG officers pass a structured selection process with criminal background checks and integrity testing before they are deployed.

Each officer completes induction training covering site procedures, supervision structures and our code of ethics. Our guarding quality management system is accredited to ISO 9001:2015.

Corporate guardingOffices, banks, factories and government premises.
Event securityExhibitions, corporate events, sports, festivals and concerts.
Private securityResidential estates, private homes and VIP protection.
Leisure securityPool and beach lifeguards and traffic controllers.

Escort security

Escort for VIPs and cash-in-transit

Our escort teams travel in private vehicles backed by a dedicated logistics network. We escort private VIP vehicles and armoured cash-in-transit vehicles, and plan every assignment case by case.

Escort vehicles send GPS data to FWG headquarters throughout the job. The command centre tracks routes, checks each step against a checklist and escalates updates in real time, so there is no single point of failure. Armed escort is available on request.

Canine unit

Patrol dogs and handlers

FWG's canine unit adds deterrence and detection to patrols on large or open sites. Our patrol dogs are trained for a different set of tasks from explosive or drug detection dogs, and always work with a trained handler.

Home / Malaysia / Electronic security

Beyond guards, gates and guns

FWG designs, installs and monitors electronic security that works with your guards. Fewer blind spots, faster response, and clear records of who came and went.

Request a quote

CCTV & video surveillance

Enterprise video security from design to installation. Cameras connect to access control, on-site alarms and two-way audio, with live streaming and video analytics. Monitor remotely on a PC or phone, with cloud storage. Careful camera placement reduces blind spots and can reduce the number of guards a site needs.

Visitor management system (iVMS)

A cloud-based system to manage visitor access and movement. Hosts pre-schedule visits, so check-in is faster and reception is less busy. Includes photo capture, visitor passes, verification and real-time reports of who is on site, exportable to PDF.

Alarms & access control

Door access, turnstiles, lift access and CCTV integrated in real time. Card issuance and biometrics let you grant and change access rights as people's roles change.

GPS tracking & command centre

Vehicles and escort teams are tracked live from FWG headquarters. The command centre monitors routes and personnel and escalates incidents as they happen.

Central monitoring (CMS)

Remote monitoring of alarms and cameras across multiple sites from one control room. To confirm: what CMS stands for and its scope
